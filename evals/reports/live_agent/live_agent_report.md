## Findings

### New voice and real-time audio models

OpenAI introduced three real-time audio models for AI agents: **GPT-Realtime-2**, **GPT-Realtime-Translate**, and **GPT-Realtime-Whisper**. Separately, OpenAI introduced **GPT-Live**, a new generation of voice models for natural human-AI interaction, now powering ChatGPT Voice; it is described as a new framework for voice interaction. OpenAI will continue supporting voice customers with access to frontier models through the OpenAI API. [2]
