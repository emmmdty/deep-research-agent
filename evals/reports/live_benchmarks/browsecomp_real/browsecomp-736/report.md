## Findings

### BRAG's Instagram Post
BRAG is a fashion and diversity brand that posted about its 2026 internship cohort on Instagram.

### Lakeshore Drive and Its Founders
Cooper Kirby, an LSA sophomore at the University of Michigan, co-founded Lakeshore Drive, a clothing brand and company, with his close friend Ryan Murray, a sophomore at Michigan State University. Murray is enrolled in MSU's Eli Broad College of Business. Lakeshore Drive works to promote "individuality and living your best life." Kirby draws inspiration from artists such as Brockhampton, Ashlinn Gray, Anderson Paak, and his community at the University. Kirby said community is the most important thing to him.

### The Local Brand
The Local Brand is a clothing brand founded by Matt Doherty, Carter Nevulis, and Aidan Manion, three Massachusetts college sophomores.

### Match Group's IPO
Match Group, Inc. began trading on the New York Stock Exchange on November 18, 2015. Its IPO price was $12 and its valuation was $2.9 billion.

### Match Group's Platforms and Ownership
Match Group owns dating platforms like Tinder and Match.com. Match Group also owns the Princeton Review test-preparation and college-counseling services.

### Match Group's User Activity (Q3 2015)
During the quarter ended September 30, 2015, Match Group's members sent an average of more than 75 million messages to other members on its products each day. Tinder had approximately 9.6 million daily active users in September 2015. Tinder users were "swiping" through an average of more than 1.4 billion user profiles each day.

### Match Group's Financial Performance (First Nine Months of 2015)
Match Group generated revenue of $752.9 million in the first nine months of 2015. Match Group earned roughly $85 million in net income in the first nine months of 2015. Match Group had more than 11.7 million paying customers by the third quarter of 2015.

### Clever NYC
Clever NYC is a streetwear brand focused on uniting people and fostering community.
