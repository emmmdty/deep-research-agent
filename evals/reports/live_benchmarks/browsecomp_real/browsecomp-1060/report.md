## Findings

### GSUTV Won Two 2020 Southeast Emmy Awards

GSUTV won two 2020 Southeast Emmy awards. One of these awards was for the documentary 'Our Plastic World,' which won first place in Documentary – Topical and Health/Science – Program Feature/Segment at the 2020 Southeast Emmys. A Georgia State University student's documentary film won a 2020 Southeast Emmy Award.

### 'Our Plastic World' Documentary Credits

The documentary 'Our Plastic World' was written and produced by Film & Media major Riũki Gakio in collaboration with Georgia State TV and Georgia Public Broadcasting.

### The Shulers Won Special Event Coverage – Live

The 2019 Georgia High School Musical Theatre Awards (The Shulers) broadcast production won first place in Special Event Coverage – Live at the 2020 Southeast Emmys.

### 2020 Southeast Emmy Ceremony Details

The 2020 Southeast Emmy winners were announced Saturday evening in an awards ceremony live streamed from Atlanta. The Regional Southeast EMMY Chapter is one of 19 chapters throughout the United States.

### 41st News and Documentary Emmy Awards

The 41st News and Documentary Emmy Awards took place on September 21-22, 2020. The nominees were announced on August 6, 2020. The winners were announced on two ceremonies via live-stream at Watch.TheEmmys.TV. The awards were presented by the National Academy of Television Arts and Sciences (NATAS), honoring the best in American news and documentary programming in 2019. NATAS recognizes excellence in television with the Emmy Award for News & Documentary, Sports, Daytime Entertainment, and other categories.

### The Salt Path Authorship Claims

The Salt Path author Raynor Winn said in a 2020 interview with Waterstones that The Salt Path was the first thing she had written since leaving school as a teenager. Winn's husband, Moth, said in the same 2020 Waterstones interview that he did not know his wife could write. Winn also said in a 2019 BBC Radio Cornwall interview that she had searched online for a literary agent.

Winn repeatedly claimed The Salt Path was the first book she had ever written. In the 2020 Waterstones interview, Winn said of The Salt Path: "It's the first thing I've written since I was a teenager leaving school – the first thing."

### The Salt Path Investigation

An investigation by The Observer in 2025 claimed The Salt Path featured misleading information about Winn's life story. Winn confirmed she wrote a secret first book despite claiming to be a debut writer.
