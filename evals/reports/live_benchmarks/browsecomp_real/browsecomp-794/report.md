## Findings

### Trisomy Definition and Chromosomal Basis
Trisomy is a genetic condition that results in an extra copy of a chromosome. Consequently, a person with trisomy will have 47 chromosomes instead of the typical 46.

### Maternal Age and Risk
Trisomy 13 and 18 are more common among parents aged 35 and older. The risk of having a child with a trisomy condition increases after age 35.

### Prevalence of Trisomy 18 and 13
Trisomy 18 is a life-threatening genetic disorder that impacts about 1 out of every 2,000 pregnancies in the U.S. However, it occurs in about one out of every 6,000 to 8,000 live births. Trisomy 13 occurs in about one out of every 8,000 to 12,000 live births.

### Prognosis and Survival
Babies with trisomy 18 or 13 disorders usually die by age 1. Many babies born with these disorders may not live beyond the first few days or weeks of life. There is no cure for trisomies 18 and 13 at this time.

### Individual Cases and Quality of Life
