## Executive Summary

- The Windsor Hotel was built in 1884 on the NE corner of Seventh and Norwood Streets and was later demolished; the first fence around Gregory Park was built in 1887.
- The Park Hotel was built in the 1870s, has 25 rooms decorated with period antiques, area rugs, and wallpaper, and was the first hotel in the local area to offer beds with spring mattresses.
- Hotel construction costs per room vary by segment (Midscale $175,000 to Luxury $550,000+), with FF&E typically accounting for 7-10% of total construction costs and furniture costs making up 30-40% of FF&E.
- Several historical theaters are documented: the Egyptian Theatre ($800,000, 1,770 seats), the Chinese Theatre ($2 million, 2,200 seats), the River Oaks Theater (retained Art Deco design), and the original Miller Outdoor Theatre (designed by William Ward Watkin, opened May 1923).
- The Plaza Hotel is located near Central Park, its design and location have generally received acclaim, and its exterior was designated by the New York City Landmarks Preservation Commission.

## Findings

### Windsor Hotel and Gregory Park
The Windsor Hotel was built on the NE corner of Seventh and Norwood Streets in 1884 and was later demolished. The first fence around Gregory Park was built in 1887.

### Park Hotel
The Park Hotel was built in the 1870s. It has 25 rooms decorated with period antiques, area rugs, and wallpaper. It was the first hotel in the local area that offered beds having spring mattresses.

### Hotel Construction Costs and FF&E
For hotel segments, average cost per room and FF&E percentages are: Luxury — $550,000 and 7%; Upper Upscale — $290,000 and 10%; Upscale — $225,000 and 10%; Midscale — $175,000 and 8%. FF&E typically accounts for 7-10% of total construction costs. Furniture costs typically make up 30-40% of FF&E.

### Estimated Furniture Costs by Hotel Segment
Estimated furniture costs per room are: Midscale ($175,000/room) — $4,200-$5,600 (3%); Upper Midscale ($205,000/room) — $6,000-$8,000 (4%); Upscale ($225,000/room) — $7,000-$9,000 (4%); Upper Upscale ($290,000/room) — $10,000-$12,000 (3.5%); Luxury ($550,000/room) — $30,000-$40,000 (2.5%).

### Price Fluctuation Factors
Hotel furniture prices fluctuate based on location, material choices, and whether furniture is mass-produced or custom-manufactured.

### Hotel Renovation Cycle
The average 8-year hotel-renovation cycle is referenced as the benchmark for product longevity.

### Theaters
The River Oaks Theater retained its Art Deco design as it appeared before construction of the adjacent shopping center in the 1940s. The original Miller Outdoor Theatre was designed by William Ward Watkin as an amphitheater surrounded by twenty Corinthian-style limestone columns and opened in May 1923. Miller Hill was created in 1948 with dirt from Fannin Street excavations. The Egyptian Theatre cost $800,000 and had 1,770 seats. The Chinese Theatre cost $2 million and had 2,200 seats. Meyer and Holler constructed four mid-sized theaters outside of Hollywood.

### Plaza Hotel
The Plaza Hotel is located near Central Park. Its design and location near Central Park has generally received acclaim. The New York City Landmarks Preservation Commission designated the Plaza Hotel's exterior.
