## Findings

### GPT-5 Launch and Capabilities

OpenAI officially launched GPT-5 on August 7, 2025 during a livestream event. GPT-5 combines advanced reasoning capabilities with multimodal processing and introduces a companion family of open-weight models called GPT-OSS. OpenAI described GPT-5 as its 'best AI system yet' and a major leap forward in reasoning, accuracy, and versatility. GPT-5 has a context length of 400K tokens (272K input + 128K output) and supports text and image modalities. It has three model tiers: GPT-5, Mini, and Nano, and is available via ChatGPT and API. GPT-5 excels in coding, writing, health, and multimodal tasks, while offering significant gains in factual reliability and safety. GPT-5 ships text, image, audio, and video input with broad tool ecosystem support. Video understanding shipped with GPT-5 in August 2025 and matured through 2026.

### GPT-5.2 Thinking Benchmark Performance

GPT-5.2 Thinking achieves 82.1% on CharXiv reasoning (no tools), while GPT-5.1 Thinking achieves 67.0%. With Python, GPT-5.2 Thinking achieves 88.7% on CharXiv reasoning, while GPT-5.1 Thinking achieves 80.3%. GPT-5.2 Thinking achieves 79.5% on MMMU Pro (no tools) and 80.4% with Python, while GPT-5.1 Thinking achieves 79.0%. On Video MMMU (no tools), GPT-5.2 Thinking achieves 85.9%, while GPT-5.1 Thinking achieves 82.9%. GPT-5.2 Thinking achieves 86.3% on Screenspot Pro (w/ Python), while GPT-5.1 Thinking achieves 64.2%. On OpenAI MRCRv2 with 8 needles at 4k-8k context, GPT-5.2 Thinking achieves 98.2%, while GPT-5.1 Thinking achieves 65.3%. GPT-5.2 leads in multimodal understanding with an 84.2% MMMU score and achieves 52.9% on ARC-AGI-2, the highest abstract reasoning score among current models.

### GPT-4o and GPT-4.1

GPT-4o launched in May of 2024 and was the first OpenAI model designed from the start to handle text, audio, and image in one model. GPT-4o's core breakthrough lies in its single integrated model architecture that can natively and end-to-end process text, audio, and visual inputs, and generate text, audio, and image outputs. This architecture significantly reduces latency, making real-time voice conversations and visual understanding possible. The 'o' in GPT-4o stands for 'omni.' GPT-4o's image generation involves a first pass of autoregressive image generation followed by a diffusion model that takes the noisy image as input instead of random noise. GPT-4.1 was released in April 2025 and supports up to 1M tokens of context length. On February 13, 2026, GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini, and o4-mini were retired from ChatGPT.

### Gemini Series

Gemini 2 was released in late 2024 and brought multimodal capabilities and context handling that matched or beat competitors in many benchmarks. Gemini 2.0 Flash was released on 30 January 2025, Gemini 2.0 Pro on 5 February 2025, and Gemini 2.0 Flash-Lite on 25 February 2025. Gemini 2.5 Pro was released on 25 March 2025. Gemini 3 is positioned as Google's most capable multimodal model to date and represents Google's largest step forward in reasoning, multimodality, enterprise reliability, and agentic capabilities. Gemini 3's performance gains over Gemini 2.5 Pro are substantial across mathematical reasoning, vision, coding, and planning. Gemini 3 is available through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, and Vertex AI. Gemini 3 Pro demonstrates strong recall, scoring 77.0% on the 128k context benchmark. Gemini 3 Pro excels in native multimodal processing with 1M token context and a 100% AIME 2025 score. Gemini 3 Deep Think holds 78.4% on Video-MME long-form, with a 7-point gap to second place (GPT-5.5 at 71.2%). The gap between Gemini 3 and second place is largest on multi-clip reasoning, temporal understanding, and tasks that require integrating across long video sequences. Gemini 2.5 Pro processes video with audio natively, while other models require separate audio-visual processing.

### Claude Series

Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4 were introduced on May 22, 2025. Both are multimodal, processing text, images, and limited video frames via a joint attention module that combines visual and text tokens in a shared latent space. They are hybrid reasoning models offering two modes: near-instant responses and extended thinking for deeper reasoning. Claude Opus 4 is described as the world's best coding model, with sustained performance on complex, long-running tasks and agent workflows. Claude Opus 4 is priced at $15/$75 per million tokens (input/output) and Sonnet 4 at $3/$15. Claude Opus 4 leads on SWE-bench (72.5%) and Terminal-bench (43.2%). Claude Sonnet 4 achieves a state-of-the-art 72.7% on SWE-bench. Claude Opus 4 scores 74.9% on GPQA Diamond without extended thinking, while Sonnet 4 scores 70.0%. Claude Opus 4 scores 87.4% on MMMLU without extended thinking, while Sonnet 4 scores 85.4%. Claude Opus 4 scores 73.7% on MMMU without extended thinking, while Sonnet 4 scores 72.6%. Claude Opus 4 achieves 79.4% on SWE-bench Verified with high compute, while Sonnet 4 achieves 80.2%. Both Claude 4 models are 65% less likely to engage in shortcut or loophole behavior than Sonnet 3.7 on agentic tasks. Claude 4 models use thinking summaries that condense lengthy thought processes with a smaller model, needed only about 5% of the time. Claude Opus 4 can create and maintain 'memory files' to store key information when given local file access. Claude Code is now generally available.

Claude Opus 4.7 was released on April 16, 2026 and is Anthropic's most capable model to date, a notable step forward for multimodal vision tasks. It ships with a higher-resolution image encoder, large gains on text-dense document and chart benchmarks, and a new tokenizer. Claude Opus 4.7 is the latest Opus-tier model and is the most capable generally available Claude model, and is the first to ship with the new cybersecurity safeguards Anthropic is developing ahead of a broader Mythos-class rollout. Claude Mythos Preview is Anthropic's most capable model and leads Opus 4.7 on most benchmarks, including SWE-bench Pro (77.8% vs 64.3%), SWE-bench Verified (93.9% vs 87.6%), Terminal-Bench (82.0% vs 69.4%), and GPQA Diamond (94.6% vs 94.2%).

### Qwen3-VL

Qwen3-VL is the most powerful vision-language model in the Qwen series to date. It is available in Dense and MoE architectures that scale from edge to cloud, with Instruct and reasoning-enhanced Thinking editions. Qwen3-VL supports native 256K context, expandable to 1M. It supports image understanding (image input with text queries) and video understanding (video input with text queries). Qwen3-VL can operate PC/mobile GUIs as a visual agent, recognizing elements, understanding functions, invoking tools, and completing tasks. It provides stronger 2D grounding and enables 3D grounding for spatial reasoning and embodied AI. Qwen3-VL supports batch inference for multiple messages and pixel control via the official Hugging Face processor.

### Multimodal Architecture Trends

Transformer plays a vital role in natural language processing (NLP) and computer vision (CV), specially for constructing large language models (LLM) and large vision models (LVM). Native multimodal training is table stakes for having a model truly at the frontier of performance and user experience. A common trend in new-generation multimodal models is the shift from 'Late Fusion' to 'Early Fusion' or even 'Joint Embedding', transforming information from different modalities into a shared semantic space at an early input stage. Another trend is end-to-end training, where the entire model from input processing to output generation is trained under a unified framework. A third trend is the evolution of attention mechanisms, including the widespread application of Cross-Attention mechanisms. GPT-4o and Gemini are no longer simple 'stitched-together' unimodal models but have undergone fundamental architectural innovations to pursue true 'native multimodal' capabilities. GPT-4o's core architecture philosophy is a single integrated model with end-to-end multimodal I/O, supporting real-time voice/visual interaction. Gemini's core architecture philosophy is built for multimodality, emphasizing deep cross-modal reasoning. Gemini was built from the ground up to be multimodal, allowing it to generalize and seamlessly understand, operate across, and combine different types of information including text, code, audio, image, and video. Native multimodal architectures are replacing bolted-on image capabilities. Dynamic resolution processing enables efficient handling of 4K images without massive token costs. Multimodal AI is now 'table stakes' for enterprise AI deployments. 2026 trends include real-time video understanding with frame-accurate analysis, on-device vision models for privacy-focused applications, vision-driven tool use without text conversion, and multi-model portfolio strategies.

### Vision Encoders

CLIP (OpenAI, 2021) pioneered contrastive vision-language pre-training. SigLIP (Google, 2023) improved efficiency with sigmoid loss. SigLIP 2 (Google, February 2025) enhanced multilingual, localization, and dense features. SigLIP 2 outperforms SigLIP at all model scales. SigLIP 2's key improvements over SigLIP include an extended training objective with multiple auxiliary losses, captioning-based pre-training, self-supervised losses (self-distillation, masked prediction), and online data curation for quality control. SigLIP 2 has multiple resolution variants preserving native aspect ratios. SigLIP 2 supports multilingual with de-biasing techniques and improved fairness across diverse populations. SigLIP 2's Global-Local Loss captures fine-grained local semantics. SigLIP 2's Masked Prediction Loss learns dense visual representations. SigLIP 2 has improved localization with better performance on object detection and segmentation. SigLIP 2 model sizes include ViT-B (86M), ViT-L (303M), ViT-So400m (400M), and ViT-g (1B) parameters.

### Model Comparisons and Recommendations

Claude 4 leads in instruction-following and long-form writing. GPT-5 is the fastest for structured output and tool use. Gemini 3 dominates multimodal tasks and offers the largest context windows. Claude dominates in safety-critical coding and autonomous agents. Gemini is the multimodal powerhouse with top vision and video. GPT-4o leads on ecosystem, Claude on safety and reasoning, and Gemini on multimodal and long-context tasks. Gemini 3 leads on raw benchmarks, but GPT-5.1 offers the best developer experience for most use cases, while Claude Sonnet 4.5 excels at careful, long-running autonomous work. OpenAI's GPT models are optimized for speed and structured output, Anthropic's Claude models offer strong reasoning with cost efficiency, and Google's Gemini models provide extended context capabilities at competitive pricing. GPT-5.4 consistently returns responses faster than Claude or Gemini at equivalent quality levels. GPT-5.4 is the most reliable for structured output, JSON, function calls, or tool use, rarely producing malformed responses. Gemini 3.1 Pro handles over 1 million tokens natively and its performance at extreme context lengths is best in class. Gemini was built multimodal from the ground up and processes images, video, and audio alongside text more naturally than competitors. Gemini 3.0 Pro is the only practical option for context windows over 200K tokens, offering a 1M context. Claude Opus 4.7 or GPT-5 are recommended for mission-critical tasks (medical, legal, financial advice) where accuracy is paramount. For sub-second latency requirements, Gemini 3.0 Flash, GPT-5-mini, and Claude Haiku 4.5 are recommended, while flagship models should be avoided. Using the Batch API for async/batch workloads (5+ seconds OK) provides a 50% cost cut, described as the single biggest LLM cost optimization most teams miss. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google all offer Batch APIs that process requests asynchronously within 24 hours and return a 50% discount on both input and output tokens.

### Self-Hosting Economics

Self-hosting a model like Llama 4 70B breaks even at approximately 500 million tokens per day with 70%+ GPU utilization sustained 365 days/year. The total annual cost of self-hosting a 4x A100 GPU instance with 70% utilization, including idle time, ML engineer, observability, storage, and failover, is estimated at $226,000-$373,000. For the annual cost of self-hosting, one could purchase approximately 283 billion Claude Haiku 4.5 input tokens (at $0.80/1M) or 3.7 trillion Gemini 3.0 Flash input tokens. Self-hosting makes sense for air-gapped/regulated environments, custom fine-tuning beyond API support, predictable high-volume workloads at 500M+ tokens/day, latency-critical edge inference, and specific open-source model capabilities.

### Embodied Intelligence and MLLMs

Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), built upon LLMs, have demonstrated impressive potential in tackling complex tasks involving visual, auditory, and textual modalities. MLLMs exhibit surprising emergent capabilities such as writing stories based on images and OCR-free multimodal tasks. Critical issues related to truthfulness, safety, o1-like reasoning, and alignment with human preference remain insufficiently addressed in MLLMs. Alignment algorithms are a powerful approach to resolving challenges of truthfulness, safety, reasoning, and human preference alignment in MLLMs. Embodied intelligent systems build upon the foundations of behavioral robotics and classical cognitive architectures, integrating multimodal perception, world modeling, and adaptive control to support closed-loop interaction in dynamic and uncertain environments. Recent breakthroughs in Multimodal Large Models (MLMs) and World Models (WMs) are profoundly transforming the field of embodied intelligence, providing tools to achieve semantic understanding and robust generalization. The review 'A review of embodied intelligence systems' (Frontiers in Robotics and AI, 06 November 2025) proposes a three-stage theoretical framework termed 'Dynamic Perception–Task Adaptation (DP-TA)' integrating multimodal perception modeling, causally driven world state prediction, and semantically guided strategy optimization. The embodied intelligence review covers key dimensions including multimodal perception, cross-modal alignment, adaptive decision-making, and Sim-to-Real transfer. 多模态大模型（如RT-2和OpenVLA）基于跨模态Transformer架构构建，能够从视觉和语言等不同输入类型创建统一表征，并进行联合推理以实现端到端控制，直接将自然语言指令转化为物理动作。世界模型旨在通过学习环境的潜在状态建立对环境的内部理解，使其能够模拟潜在的未来状态并建模因果关系。多模态大模型与世界模型的结合带来显著益处，包括提高系统通用性、支持跨任务学习，以及促进从仿真到现实的部署（Sim-to-Real）。当前具身智能研究仍存在重要空白：没有完整框架能系统整合所有组件，信息耦合、模块解耦和接口设计等问题仍未解决。该综述提出了具身智能系统的结构化建模框架DP-TA（动态感知-任务适应）三层融合架构，将具身系统分解为三个核心功能层：多模态感知与对齐、世界建模与任务图生成、策略适应与执行调度。该研究识别的当前研究核心挑战和未来趋势包括：语义一致性建模、多策略架构融合、轻量级世界模型构建和多任务适应。现代具身环境需要视觉、语言、触觉和深度信息等异构模态数据的复杂协同整合。多模态感知的核心挑战已提升至跨模态语义融合、互补信息建模、动态时间对齐以及环境扰动和传感器不确定性下的鲁棒性。语言、视觉和动作联合表征理论的突破使多模态感知成为实现语言指令解析、环境状态反馈和自适应任务规划之间闭环协作的关键使能技术。OmniSegmentor框架展示了跨五种视觉模态（RGB、深度、热成像等）的通用预训练范式，显著提升了感知能力，并在多个分割基准上创下新的最先进记录。多模态感知已成为具身智能系统的核心输入组件，但感知不确定性建模和跨模态语义对齐仍是关键挑战。感知数据中的不确定性可分为两类，第一类源于感知数据本身固有的模糊性、不完整性和环境干扰（如运动模糊、传感器噪声和局部遮挡），被称为内在不确定性，具有不可修复的性质。
