## Executive Summary

- Bartłomiej Kasprzykowski played the titular Roman (Ray) in the Polish-language adaptation of Everybody Loves Raymond, titled Wszyscy kochają Romana. [10]
- In the Polish adaptation Wszyscy kochają Romana, Bartłomiej Kasprzykowski is credited as Roman (Ray). [10]
- Ray Romano played Raymond 'Ray' Barone on the CBS sitcom Everybody Loves Raymond (1996–2005). [2]
- Everybody Loves Roman is a Polish-language TV series released September 2, 2011 in Poland. [3]
- The Polish adaptation Wszyscy kochają Romana premiered on TVN on 2 September 2011. [10]

## Findings

### Finding 1: Original U.S. Series Casting
Ray Romano played Raymond 'Ray' Barone on the CBS sitcom *Everybody Loves Raymond* (1996–2005). This is confirmed by evidence directly stating the actor and role.

### Finding 2: Polish Adaptation Title
The Polish-language version of *Everybody Loves Raymond* is titled *Wszyscy kochają Romana*, not *Wszyscy kochają Raymonda*. The claim that the title was *Wszyscy kochają Raymonda* is contradicted by evidence indicating the adaptation is titled *Wszyscy kochają Romana* (as referenced by the titular Roman).

### Finding 3: Polish Adaptation Casting
Bartłomiej Kasprzykowski played the titular Roman (Ray) in the Polish-language adaptation *Wszyscy kochają Romana*. He is credited as "Roman (Ray)" in the adaptation.

### Finding 4: Polish Adaptation Premiere Date
The Polish adaptation *Wszyscy kochają Romana* premiered on TVN on 2 September 2011. The claim that the Polish-language version aired from 1996 to 2005 is contradicted; those dates apply to the original American series, not the Polish version.

### Finding 5: Unsupported Identification Claim
The claim that the actor who played Ray in the Polish-language version was not identified in the provided excerpts is accepted as accurately reflecting the excerpts' lack of identification. However, this is superseded by the accepted claim naming Bartłomiej Kasprzykowski as the actor.

### Finding 6: Unrelated Polish Series
Magda M. is a Polish television series whose main roles were played by Joanna Brodzik and Paweł Małaszyński. [16] This is unrelated to the *Everybody Loves Raymond* adaptations but is confirmed by evidence.

## Evidence Status

- **Accepted claims:** Ray Romano's role in the original series; the Polish adaptation title *Wszyscy kochają Romana*; Bartłomiej Kasprzykowski's role as Roman (Ray); the premiere date of 2 September 2011 on TVN; the lack of identification in the excerpts; and the casting of *Magda M.*
- **Contradicted claims:** The Polish title *Wszyscy kochają Raymonda* (contradicted by evidence of *Wszyscy kochają Romana*); the Polish version airing from 1996 to 2005 (contradicted by the 2011 premiere date).
- **No unsupported claims** were included in the final report; all claims are either accepted or contradicted per critic decisions.
