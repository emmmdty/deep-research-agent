### Finding 2: Quoted Writer
The writer quoted by Merriam-Webster for the Word of the Day entry from June 27, 2022, was Annie Levin. This is supported by accepted claims (03, 04, 06, 10, 14) that attribute the quote to her.

### Finding 3: Source of the Quote
The quote from Annie Levin appeared in *The New York Observer* on March 7, 2022. This is stated in accepted claim 07.

### Finding 4: Definition of Jingoism
Jingoism is defined as excessive patriotism or nationalism, especially when marked by a belligerent foreign policy. This definition is provided in accepted claims (08, 11, 15).

### Finding 5: Origin of Jingoism
Jingoism originated during the Russo-Turkish War of 1877–1878. This is stated in accepted claims (12, 16). Note that claim 12 specifies only "the Russo-Turkish War," while claim 16 provides the fuller date range "1877–1878."
