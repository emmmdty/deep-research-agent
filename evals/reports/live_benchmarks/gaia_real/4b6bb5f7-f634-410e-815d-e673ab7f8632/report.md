## Executive Summary

- In *Doctor Who* Series 9, Episode 11, titled "Heaven Sent," the Doctor is trapped inside an ever-shifting maze located within a waterlocked castle.
- The castle prison is revealed to be the Doctor's confession dial, made bigger on the inside using time or technology.
- The episode is set entirely inside the confession dial, which the Doctor describes as "a fully automated haunted house. A mechanical maze" and "a killer puzzle box designed to scare me to death."
- The Doctor was imprisoned in the castle by his people, the Time Lords.
- The episode originally aired on 28 Nov 2015.

## Findings

### Episode Title and Airdate
The episode is titled "Heaven Sent" and originally aired on 28 Nov 2015. The episode is set inside the confession dial.

### The Doctor's Imprisonment
The Doctor is trapped inside an ever-shifting maze in a waterlocked castle, imprisoned by his people, the Time Lords. He is trapped inside an empty castle, which is described as a "killer puzzle box" in the episode.

### The Confession Dial
The castle is revealed to be the Doctor's confession dial. The location where the Doctor is trapped in the ever-shifting maze is called the "confession dial" in the episode. The castle prison was the Doctor's confession dial, made bigger on the inside using time or technology.

### The Doctor's Descriptions of the Location
The Doctor describes the location as "a fully automated haunted house. A mechanical maze." He also describes it as "a killer puzzle box designed to scare me to death, and I'm trapped inside it."

### Opening Scene and Teleport Chamber
The first scene heading in the script is "[Teleport chamber room]." The Doctor appears in a teleport chamber after a blackened hand pulls a lever and turns to dust. He determines the equipment in the room is consistent with an augmented ultra long-range teleport. The Doctor says he is not more than a single light year from where he was and is in the same time zone.

### The Maze and Its Features
The Doctor encounters a hunchbacked figure in pale grey veils, referred to as the Veil of the cast list. He opens a door to reveal a solid wall just a pace behind it. The Doctor sees sections of the walls rotating in opposite directions with the uppermost faster than the lower ones. He enters a room revealed after the walls rotate, with the scene heading "[Bedroom]." In the bedroom, the Doctor sees a portrait of Clara Oswald hanging over the fireplace. The Doctor dives through a broken window and bursts into the Tardis. The Doctor walks through the great hall of Caerphilly Castle.

## Evidence Status

- **Accepted claims:** All findings above are based on accepted claims, with the exception of two qualified claims.
- **Qualified claims:**
  - The claim that the maze is described as an "ever-shifting maze" in the episode is qualified: the source says "this energy shifting maze," not "ever-shifting maze," so the exact phrase is not supported.
  - The claim that the location in Series 9, Episode 11 where the Doctor is trapped in an ever-shifting maze is called "Heaven Sent" in the official script is qualified: the source only says "The Doctor" and does not support the claim that the location is called "Heaven Sent" in the script.
- **Unsupported claims:** None beyond the qualified claims noted above.
