## Findings

### Freon-12 Identity and Chemical Formula
Freon-12 (R-12) is dichlorodifluoromethane, with the chemical formula CCl2F2. It is a chlorofluorocarbon with a molecular mass of 120.91 g/mol (one source gives 120.93 g/mol).

### Mariana Trench Environmental Conditions
The temperature at the bottom of the Mariana Trench is 1 to 4 °C (34 to 39 °F). The water column above exerts a pressure of 1,086 bar (15,750 psi). This pressure is approximately 1,071.8 times standard atmospheric pressure at sea level, described in other sources as "about a thousand times" or "almost 1,000 times" atmospheric pressure. The water is consistently just above freezing, typically 1° to 4°, and is described as "just a few degrees above freezing."

### Freon-12 Physical Properties
Freon-12 has a molecular weight, boiling point, critical pressure, critical temperature, and critical density as physical properties. [2] Specific values include:
- Boiling point: -29.75°C at one atmosphere
- Critical temperature: 111.97°C (another source: 112 °C)
- Critical pressure: 4136 kPa (another source: 41.15 bar)
- Fluid density at critical conditions: 565.0 kg/m³
- Critical density: 4.70 mol/l (per Okada, Uematsu, et al., 1986)

### Freon-12 Liquid Density at Various Temperatures
Dichlorodifluoromethane liquid density values are documented:
- At 1.013 bar at boiling point: 1486 kg/m³
- At 250 K: 1468 kg/m³
- At 300 K: 1304 kg/m³
- At -50°C: 1546.75 kg/m³
- At -40°C: 1518.71 kg/m³
- At -30°C: 1489.56 kg/m³

### Freon-12 Saturation Property Tables
Freon-12 saturation property tables exist covering temperatures from -100 to -44 degrees Celsius. The specific volume of saturated liquid Freon-12 is tabulated in units of m³/kg.

### Marianas Trench Peak Conditions Not Provided
The source excerpts do not provide peak temperature and pressure conditions for the Marianas Trench.

## Evidence Status

All claims were accepted based on direct evidence in the source excerpts. One claim (regarding the absence of Marianas Trench peak conditions) was accepted as supported because the evidence span contained only a heading and no such data. No claims were rejected or marked as unsupported. Minor discrepancies exist between sources for molecular weight (120.91 vs. 120.93 g/mol) and critical pressure (4136 kPa vs. 41.15 bar), but both values are reported as accepted.
