## Findings

### Artist and Title
The artwork with accession number 2022.128 is by Buck Ellison. Its full title is 'Rain in Rifle Season, Distributions from Split-Interest Trusts, Price Includes Uniform, Never Hit Soft, 2003' (dated 2021). A shorter title, 'Rain in Rifle Season', is also attested for the same accession number. Ellison is a Los Angeles-based conceptual photographer.

### Subject and Source Material
The photograph depicts Erik Prince, reimagined as he might have been around the start of the war in Afghanistan. Prince co-founded the security and mercenary firm Blackwater and was a former Navy SEAL. The photograph is based on Prince's book 'Civilian Warriors', an autobiography he wrote to clear his name. The photograph shows a person holding a book by Carl von Clausewitz titled *On War* (1832).

### Physical and Edition Details
The artwork is an archival pigment print measuring 40 × 53 1/4 inches (101.6 × 135.3 cm) for both sheet and image. It is an edition of 1/5 with 2 artist's proofs.

### Acquisition and Exhibition
The artwork was purchased with funds from the Photography Committee. It was exhibited in the Whitney Biennial 2022 at the Whitney Museum of American Art, New York, through 5 September.

### Related Series
Buck Ellison's *Performance Fleece* (2018) series photographs a hearty blond man modelling a range of fleeces from Vineyard Vines, a brand of 'preppy casual clothing' based in Martha's Vineyard.

## Evidence Status

All claims were accepted by the critic. The following qualifications apply:

- **Title discrepancy**: Claims 01 and 04 attest the short title 'Rain in Rifle Season'; claims 08, 13, 17, and 22 attest the full title 'Rain in Rifle Season, Distributions from Split-Interest Trusts, Price Includes Uniform, Never Hit Soft, 2003'. Both are accepted; the full title is more specific and includes the date element '2003' within the title string, while the artwork itself is dated 2021 (claim 17).
- **Medium**: Claim 14 states the artwork is an archival pigment print; the critic noted the medium is "implied by the context" rather than directly stated in the evidence span.
- **Exhibition**: Claims 10, 15, and 25 confirm the work was on view in the Whitney Biennial 2022; claim 25 specifies the closing date of 5 September.
- **Dimensions**: Claims 03 and 05 both confirm the sheet dimensions; claim 05 additionally confirms the image dimensions are identical.
