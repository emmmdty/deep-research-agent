## Executive Summary

- The Book of Esther (NIV) first mentions a place by name; the location of the events is present-day Iran. [6]
- Ahasuerus was a great king of Persia.
- Amīr ʿAbbas Hoveyda was the prime minister of Iran under Shah Mohammed Reza Pahlavi from January 1965 to August 1977. [9]
- Hoveyda's tenure as prime minister ended in August 1977, meaning he was no longer prime minister in April 1977. [9]
- The events of the Book of Esther fit chronologically between the 6th and 7th chapters of Ezra. [6]
