## Findings

### Flavor Graveyard Origins and Location

Ben & Jerry's constructed the Flavor Graveyard in 1997, beginning with only four monuments. The original four flavors were Dastardly Mash, Economic Crunch, Ethan Almond, and Tuskegee Chunk. The graveyard has grown to include over 35 flavors, with one source noting 40 flavors at the time of a specific visit.

The Flavor Graveyard is an actual graveyard onsite at Ben & Jerry's with granite headstones and witty epitaphs. It is located in Waterbury, Vermont, near the factory. More specifically, it is located at the top of the property in the upper parking lots, past the playground area, on a picturesque hill overlooking the factory. The Ben & Jerry's factory address is 1281 Waterbury Stowe Rd Route 100, Waterbury, VT 05676. The Flavor Graveyard exists in two incarnations: on Ben & Jerry's websites around the world, and also at the Ben & Jerry's factory in Waterbury, Vermont. The graveyard is closed in the winter season due to its remote location on property and the hazardous winter terrain.

### Epitaphs and Flavor Details

Each headstone in the Flavor Graveyard features a rhyming epitaph, with a headstone dedicated to each flavor, reminding visitors of what the ice cream was made of and providing an epitaph befitting each flavor.

**Dastardly Mash:** The headstone epitaph reads: "Here the brazen DASTARDLY lies. Some say that raisin, Caused its demise." (Note: evidence only partially supports this full text; see Evidence Status.) Dastardly Mash was available from 1979-1991, though the headstone says 1976.

**Oh Pear:** The Oh Pear epitaph reads: "Oh Pear, Oh Pear, / A mixture of mirth / All nannies did weep / When you left of this earth." The last line of the Oh Pear epitaph is "When you left of this earth." Oh Pear was available in 1997. The Oh Pear flavor description is "Fresh pear ice cream with a hint of almond and a light fudge swirl."

**Turtle Soup:** The Turtle Soup epitaph reads: "Slow and Steady wins the race, Turtle Soup kept up the pace. You may protest, but we suggest Turtle Soup deserves a rest." (Note: evidence only partially supports this full text; see Evidence Status.) Turtle Soup was available from 2006-2010.

**Other Flavors:** Flavors in the Flavor Graveyard include Oh Pear (1997), Makin' Whoopie Pie (2002-2003), and Urban Jumble (2000-2001). Aloha Macadamia was a flavor in the Flavor Graveyard from 2001-2002. The Sugar Plum flavor description is "Plum ice cream with a caramel swirl."

### The Last Line of the Rhyme on the Oldest Flavor's Headstone

The last line of the rhyme under the flavor name on the headstone visible in the background of the photo of the oldest flavor's headstone in the Ben & Jerry's online flavor graveyard as of the end of 2022 is "Well, maybe not." The full epitaph is: "Peanuts, Popcorn! Mix 'em in a pot! Plop 'em in your ice cream! Well, maybe not."

## Evidence Status

- **Accepted claims:** The vast majority of claims were accepted based on direct evidence support. These include the graveyard's construction date (1997), original four flavors, location details, the Oh Pear epitaph and its last line, the Dastardly Mash availability period, and the last line "Well, maybe not" for the oldest flavor's headstone.

- **Qualified claims:** Several claims were qualified because the evidence only partially supported them:
  - The full epitaphs for Dastardly Mash (claims 03, 26) and Turtle Soup (claims 05, 28) were only partially supported by evidence fragments; the full text as claimed was not provided in the evidence.
  - Claims 37-50 each combined an availability period with an epitaph ending. The evidence only contained the epitaph ending, not the availability period. Therefore, the epitaph endings are supported, but the availability periods are not supported by the evidence.

- **Contradicted claim:** Claim 31, which stated the last line was "Mac got aloha'ed off the aisle," was contradicted by evidence supporting claim 51, which provides the full epitaph ending with "Well, maybe not."
