## Findings

### Virtue Restaurant's Identity and Location
Virtue Restaurant & Bar is a Hyde Park restaurant in Chicago, located at 1462 E 53rd Street. [2] It is the brainchild of James Beard Award-winning chef Erick Williams.

### Menu Items (Current/General)
The restaurant's menu includes:
- Pancetta & Asparagus Gnocchi
- Lamb Kofta
- Pappardelle with duck
- Wood Roasted Oyster
- King Salmon
- Amberjack Crudo
- Focaccia Bread with anchovy butter
- Fried green tomatoes with tangy remoulade
- Biscuits described as fluffy yet crispy with a honey glaze
- Gumbo

### Wine List
The menus page includes wine listings, such as 'l'éspirit des pavots' estate red, Napa Valley 2021.

### Surcharge Policy
Virtue Restaurant adds a 3.5% surcharge to the final bill to assist in covering the costs of providing health insurance and benefits to the team. [5,11]

### King Salmon Dish Details
Virtue serves a king salmon dish encrusted with herbs including chives, basil, parsley, and tarragon, accompanied by corn, hominy, red bell peppers, and butter.

### Dinner Menu Large Rations (with Prices)
The dinner menu includes the following large rations:
- Soft shell crab – $36
- Blackened catfish – $30 (also reported at $69 in another claim)
- Salmon – $43
- Half chicken – $29
- Short rib – $42 (also reported at $29 in another claim)
- NY strip steak – $69 (also reported at $42 in another claim)
- Fish & grits – $35
- Cabbage – $26

### Dinner Menu Items (Descriptions and Prices)
- Short rib with creamed spinach, potatoes, and caramelized onion gravy – $29 (conflicting with $42 above)
- NY strip steak with bone marrow butter and chermoula – $42 (conflicting with $69 above)
- Blackened catfish with Carolina gold rice and barbeque carrots – $69 (conflicting with $30 above)

### Extra Rations
The dinner menu includes extra rations such as buttered grits, crushed potatoes, collards & smoked turkey, mac & cheese, and cornbread & honey butter.

### Cocktails
The menu includes:
- Apple Jack – apple brandy, lemon, raspberry – $16
- All Right – bourbon, curacao, aromatic bitters – $16
- Blackthorne – with sloe – $16

### Archived March 2021 Menu (Wayback Machine)
The archived menu included:
- Pancetta & Asparagus Gnocchi
- Lamb Kofta
- Caviar and Chips
- Deviled Eggs
- Spring Citrus & Kale Salad
- Roasted Cauliflower
- Tuna Crudo
- Shrimp Saganaki
- Wagyu Carpaccio
- Pistachio Cheesecake
- Baklava

### Multiple Locations
Virtue Restaurant has three locations, and the menu shown should be looked for at the East Tremont location.

## Evidence Status

All claims were accepted by the critic. However, several claims contain conflicting price information for the same menu items:
- Short rib: $29 (claim 14) vs. $42 (claim 41)
- NY strip steak: $42 (claim 15) vs. $69 (claim 42)
- Blackened catfish: $69 (claim 16) vs. $30 (claim 38)

These discrepancies are noted but not resolved within the provided claims. All other claims are accepted as stated, with no hedged or qualified language present in the source material.
