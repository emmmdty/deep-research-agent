## Executive Summary

- Virtue Restaurant & Bar is a Hyde Park restaurant in Chicago, located at 1462 E 53rd Street, founded by James Beard Award-winning chef Erick Williams.
- The restaurant's menu includes a range of dishes, from appetizers like Pancetta & Asparagus Gnocchi and Focaccia Bread with anchovy butter to large rations such as short rib, NY strip steak, and blackened catfish.
- A 3.5% surcharge is added to the final bill to help cover health insurance and benefits for the team.
- An archived March 2021 menu (via Wayback Machine) lists several items, including Lamb Kofta, Caviar and Chips, and Pistachio Cheesecake.
- There is a discrepancy in reported prices for some dinner items (e.g., short rib listed at both $29 and $42; blackened catfish at both $69 and $30), which remains unresolved.

## Findings

### Virtue Restaurant's Identity and Location
Virtue Restaurant & Bar is a Hyde Park restaurant in Chicago, located at 1462 E 53rd Street. It is the brainchild of James Beard Award-winning chef Erick Williams.

### Surcharge Policy
Virtue Restaurant adds a 3.5% surcharge to the final bill to assist in covering the costs of providing health insurance and benefits to the team.

### King Salmon Dish Details

## Evidence Status

All claims were accepted by the critic. However, several claims contain conflicting price information for the same menu items:
- Short rib: $29 (claim 14) vs. $42 (claim 41)
- NY strip steak: $42 (claim 15) vs. $69 (claim 42)
- Blackened catfish: $69 (claim 16) vs. $30 (claim 38)

These discrepancies are noted but not resolved within the provided claims. All other claims are accepted as stated, with no hedged or qualified language present in the source material.
